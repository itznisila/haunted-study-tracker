# Product: Haunted Study Tracker

A study tracker for university students where procrastination literally haunts you.
Built for the Kiroween 2026 hackathon, **Costume Contest** category.

## The core idea
- Every assignment is a task with a deadline.
- When a task becomes overdue it turns into a **ghost** that drifts around the screen.
  The longer it is overdue, the bigger, darker and angrier the ghost becomes.
- Completing a task **exorcises** its ghost with a satisfying animation.
- A Pomodoro focus timer is a **candle** that burns down while you study.
- Your daily study streak is shown as the brightness of the room: study more, the haunting fades.

## Design rule (most important)
Spooky elements must **carry information**, never just decorate.
Every effect should answer a question for the user: "What is overdue? How badly? Am I focused? Am I on a streak?"
If an effect does not tell the user something, remove it.

## Users
University students who juggle assignments, labs and exams and tend to leave things late.

## Non-goals
- No accounts, login or server. Everything stays in the browser.
- No social features, no AI features.
